# Bail Guard with Zing electronic stumps and bails

Does Bail Guard work with Zing electronic (LED) bails? This folder has the
test procedure, what we saw, and the measurements. The short answer is on the
[website FAQ](https://cricketbailguard.org/faq.html#zing).

Result: Bail Guard is compatible with Club Zing (Series 1) electronic bails.
The bails dislodge and light up normally with the device fitted.

- **Video:** https://youtu.be/USnq6s3Hpyo
- **When:** September to October 2026
- **Equipment:** Club Zing (Series 1) stumps and bails, on loan from Minor
  League Cricket (MiLC)

## Scope

Read this before you use the data.

- This was a compatibility check. It was not an impact test or a safety-force
  test.
- We tested Club Zing (Series 1). This is the club-level system. We did not
  test the professional Zing system used in international and IPL matches. We
  would welcome the chance to test it.
- No endorsement or approval is claimed by any party.

## Procedure

We fitted Bail Guard to the Zing wicket. Then we threw a ball at the wicket.
These are the checks we made:

1. The ball just clips one bail.
2. One bail is dislodged.
3. Both bails are dislodged.
4. One bail is dislodged, then the other on a second throw.

These were informal checks. They are a sample, not a full test series.

## What we saw

In each case, the Zing bails dislodged and lit up normally. There was no
difference with Bail Guard fitted.

## Measurements

Club Zing (Series 1) parts, with one wooden bail for comparison. The same data
is in [zing-measurements.csv](zing-measurements.csv).

| Part | Mass (g) |
|---|---|
| Wooden bail (one sample, for comparison) | 25.0 |
| Zing bail, with battery | 51.4 |
| Zing bail, without battery | 43.0 |
| Zing bail battery | 8.3 |
| Zing stump, with battery | 820.7 |
| Zing stump battery | 41.4 |

The stump battery is a different battery from the bail battery.

- A Zing bail with its battery is about twice the mass of the wooden bail we weighed:
  51.4 g compared with 25.0 g.
- The MCC Laws of Cricket set the size of bails and stumps, but not their
  mass.
- In this setup, the collar rests on the lower stopper, so it adds no load to
  the bails.
