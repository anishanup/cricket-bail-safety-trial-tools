# Bail Guard Impact from DCL Scorecards

**Week:** August 1 to August 5, 2026 (DCL Wk6)  
**Source:** Dallas Cricket League official scorecards (dallascricket.org)

This counts the dismissals that physically dislodge the bails, which are the moments a bail guard device would be engaged. **Total** = Bowled + Stumped + Run out + Hit wicket.

On average about **5.0 bail-dislodging dismissals per game.**

> **Important: this is a lower bound.** These counts come only from completed *dismissals* on the scorecard. They do **not** capture the many other times the bails are dislodged during a game, for example:
> - Run-out and stumping *attempts* where the batter was not out (the keeper or fielder still breaks the stumps)
> - A bowled or hit-wicket off a no-ball (the bails come off but it is not a dismissal)
> - Any other ball that hits the stumps without a wicket being given
>
> The real number of bail-guard impacts per game is therefore higher than shown here. Those non-dismissal dislodges are not in the scorecard data and have to be captured separately, through notes from game participants (umpires, scorers, players).

## Notes

- The table lists every Wk6 game that was played to a result **and** has ball-by-ball scoring entered, grouped by the league's official week number (**Wk6**), across 3 tournaments.
- 3 Wk6 fixtures had a scorecard with no batting detail entered (abandoned, conceded, or result-only) and are excluded: Daring Bering vs NORTEK TRIUMPHS XI (DCL Fall Tournament, 2026-08-02); Dallas Old School vs Legacy Titans (DLCL Fall T20 Tournament, 2026-08-02); Linkwell Velociraptors vs Tech Warriors (DCL Fall Tournament, 2026-08-02).
- **Fire Flyers vs VR Warriors** (2026-07-29) is recorded by the league as a Wk6 fixture but was played outside the main weekend; it is included here to match the league's week grouping.
- The published schedule lists more fixtures; any game that was not played, or whose scorecard was never entered, is not counted here.
- **Run out** is the combined count. DCL scorecards do not record whether a run out was a direct hit or a relayed (indirect) run out, so that split is not available from this data.
- Caught, caught-behind and LBW dismissals are excluded because they do not disturb the stumps.
